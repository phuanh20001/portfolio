Muster POS ichicafekippax.com · github.com/phuanh20001/Muster-POS
Point-of-sale & online-ordering platform running the counter at Ichi Cafe, Kippax ACT
  • Three card processors behind one interface (Stripe, Square, and a direct bank terminal), so checkout, split-tender and refunds behave identically whichever is active; each order records which processor charged it, so a refund always routes back correctly.
  • Integrated a CommBank EFTPOS terminal at the protocol level rather than through an SDK: binary TCP/IP framing via Linkly's on-premises client, mid-sale prompts such as signature approval answered from the till, and a durable in-flight record so a crash mid-transaction is recovered on boot. Built and submitted for Linkly accreditation.
  • Exact money handling with decimal.js throughout, never floating-point cents; server-side price recomputation on every online order, plus a reconciliation report flagging any order that drifts from the processor by more than a cent.
  • Hard LAN/public trust boundary: POS and admin stay on the shop network, only customer routes reach the internet via a Cloudflare Tunnel; offline-tolerant PWA keeps cash sales working when the internet drops.
Stack: Next.js 16, JavaScript, PostgreSQL, Prisma, Tailwind, Stripe, Square, Linkly / PC-EFTPOS
